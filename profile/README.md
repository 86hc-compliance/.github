### 🏛️ 关于 86HC / About 86HC

**86HC (86hc.com)** 是专注于香港法团治理、商业大数据合规检索与反洗钱尽职审查（AML/KYC Due Diligence）的专业服务平台。我们严格遵循香港《公司条例》（第622章）与《商业登记条例》（第310章），为全球投资机构、银行风控、跨境律所及出海企业提供法定、可溯源的商业合规数据支持。

86HC is an institutional-grade platform delivering authoritative corporate intelligence, statutory records retrieval, and AML/KYC compliance verification for Hong Kong corporations. Operating under the official Trust or Company Service Provider license (TCSP: TC006431), we bridge global compliance standards with Hong Kong legal registries.
